Our Vision

Marble has been part of the human story for centuries, often reserved for the monumental and the grand. At Argiri, we bring it closer — shaping it into objects that belong to your everyday spaces.

Each piece is designed to be lived with, to quietly integrate into daily rituals, and to offer a sense of material depth beyond its form. We are building a world that exists to make marble intimate — where a small marble piece, placed with care, carries as much presence as an entire room.

Argiri is for everyone who believes and chooses to live with intention.

"Where material beauty is felt quietly, personally, and with full intention."

Argiri sources, crafts, and offers marble in every form — from sculptural objects to full stone slabs — for those who see material not just as surface, but as something to be lived with.

Design Philosophy

We design for the pause.

I — Intent

Chosen with care

Every tone, texture, and inlay is chosen with purpose — whether quiet or expressive. We work with the material, not over it, letting its depth and character lead.

II — Balance

Structure meets fluidity

Balance defines our approach — between structure and fluidity, surface and texture, presence and subtlety. Each decision preserves clarity and character.

III — Patience

Unhurried, considered

We move with patience. Each object is created to settle into its space with ease, revealing itself over time rather than demanding attention at once.

What We Stand For

Marble that meets you

Sometimes marble commands a room. Sometimes it catches the light on a quiet afternoon and stops you mid-thought. Argiri exists in that space — where material and moment find each other.

One material, every world

Marble has lived in temples, in palaces, in the quietest corners of the most personal homes. It has never belonged to just one kind of space or one kind of person. Neither do we — Argiri is as much at home in a grand lobby as it is on a bedside table, because great material has no limits.

Before it reaches you

Every piece is held before it is offered — turned, observed, and chosen not just for how it looks, but for how it feels. We work with a quiet sense of balance, something instinctive rather than defined. We take our time, so that by the time it reaches you, it feels certain.

The Argiri Feeling
Marble has a temperature. A weight. An emotion. When you hold it, something in you settles.

That is the "Argiri feeling" — the moment an object reminds you to be present. Not nostalgia. Not luxury for its own sake. Just the simple, profound experience of holding something real. And then it becomes yours.

Marble is not one thing. It is not just white, cold and silent. It is green, beige, black, rose, grey — veined and alive, each piece unrepeatable. It sits as naturally beside colorful beads and bold designs as it does in a quiet minimalist room. It doesn't ask you to change your world. It asks to be part of it, exactly as it is.

The pause belongs to the marble. Everything after belongs to you.

Marble Care

Living with marble, well.

  • 01 Wipe spills promptly — acidic liquids like citrus, wine, and vinegar can etch the surface if left to sit.
  • 02 Clean with a soft cloth and a pH-neutral stone soap; avoid vinegar, bleach, and other acidic or abrasive household cleaners.
  • 03 Reseal periodically to help guard against staining, especially on frequently used surfaces.
  • 04 Use coasters and trivets under glasses, bottles, and hot cookware to prevent rings and heat marks.
  • 05 Dust sculptural pieces regularly with a dry, soft cloth to preserve their natural polish over time.
